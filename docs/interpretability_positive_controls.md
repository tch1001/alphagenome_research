# AlphaGenome mechanistic-interpretability positive controls

## Purpose and scope

This protocol asks whether causal interpretability methods can recover known
cis-regulatory or splicing mechanisms from AlphaGenome. It deliberately starts
with mechanisms established by experiments, then freezes the analysis before
applying it to less-understood loci.

The claim under test is limited to AlphaGenome's computation:

> An internal intervention identifies how AlphaGenome produces a prediction.

It does **not** by itself establish that a cell uses the same mechanism. A model
mechanism becomes biological evidence only when it agrees with an independent
perturbation experiment.

## Ranked control ladder

| Rank | Role | System | Why it is included |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Tooling control** | COL6A2 splice variant | Exact, local and visually unambiguous test of activation capture, patching and ablation. |
| 0 | **Tooling control** | TAL1 neo-enhancer | Exact multimodal example for testing motif-to-enhancer-to-promoter instrumentation. |
| 1 | **Substantive evaluation** | BCL11A +58 enhancer | Experimentally dissected GATA1-dependent enhancer with local, transcriptional and distal cis readouts. |
| 2 | **Substantive evaluation** | OpenSplice | Large 2026 temporal holdout with measured variant-level changes in exon inclusion. |
| 3 | Extension | Fulco/Gasperini K562 CRISPRi | Systematic enhancer-to-gene effects and distance-matched negatives after the method is frozen. |

COL6A2 and TAL1 are development fixtures, not independent discoveries. Results
on them must never be counted as substantive biological validation.

## Tooling controls

### COL6A2 donor-switch control

- Assembly and variant: GRCh38 `chr21:46126238:G>C`.
- Gene and context: `COL6A2`, aorta.
- Established result: the variant disrupts the extant donor, creates a new
  donor, produces a novel splice junction and extends the exon. The altered
  RNA-seq coverage was observed in a heterozygous GTEx sample.
- AlphaGenome outputs:
  - tissue-agnostic `splice_sites`;
  - aorta `splice_site_usage`;
  - aorta `splice_junctions`;
  - aorta RNA-seq.
- Expected direction:
  - donor/site-usage signal decreases at the reference donor;
  - donor/site-usage signal increases at the alternative donor;
  - the reference junction weakens and the alternative junction strengthens;
  - ALT RNA coverage extends to the alternative exon boundary.
- Initial context: 131,072 bp. A shorter supported context may be used while
  debugging, but the final tooling result must be repeated at 131,072 bp.

This is an explicit AlphaGenome paper example and is therefore unsuitable as
evidence of generalization or novelty.

### TAL1 neo-enhancer control

- Assembly and variant: GRCh38 `chr1:47239296:C>ACG`.
- Gene and context: `TAL1`; the altered element is approximately 7.5 kb from
  the TAL1 transcription start site. Use CD34-positive common myeloid
  progenitor tracks, matching the published AlphaGenome analysis.
- Established result: the insertion creates a de novo MYB motif. Experiments
  show MYB recruitment, enhancer formation and increased TAL1 expression;
  disruption of the mutant element collapses MYB/H3K27ac and TAL1 expression.
- AlphaGenome outputs: DNase, H3K27ac, H3K4me1, H3K4me3, H3K9me3, H3K27me3,
  H3K36me3 and TAL1 RNA-seq.
- Expected direction:
  - increased local accessibility and active histone marks at the insertion;
  - decreased repressive marks near the TAL1 promoter;
  - increased TAL1 RNA prediction;
  - attribution localizes to the inserted MYB motif.
- Initial context: 131,072 bp; confirm the final result at 1,048,576 bp.

This variant, its predicted effects and its in-silico-mutagenesis motif were
already shown by AlphaGenome. It is only an end-to-end instrumentation control.

## Substantive evaluation 1: BCL11A +58 enhancer

### Frozen biological hypothesis

Disrupting the GATA1 motif in the erythroid BCL11A +58 enhancer should produce
the following cis-regulatory chain inside AlphaGenome:

```text
GATA1-motif disruption
  -> reduced local GATA1/enhancer activity
  -> reduced enhancer transcription
  -> reduced enhancer-to-BCL11A-promoter contribution
  -> reduced BCL11A transcription
```

AlphaGenome cannot test the later trans-regulatory consequence, increased
`HBG1`/`HBG2` and fetal haemoglobin, because those genes are on chromosome 11
and outside the chromosome-2 input window.

### Locus and perturbations

- Assembly: GRCh38.
- sg1617 target: `chr2:60495264-60495283`.
- E+58 enhancer RNA TSS: `chr2:60495297`.
- Primary perturbation: the published one-base A insertion into the GATA1
  motif generated by sg1617 editing.
- Replication perturbations: the published 13-bp and 15-bp deletions overlapping
  that motif.
- Negative sequence controls:
  - equal-length edits outside the GATA1 motif but inside E+58;
  - dinucleotide-preserving shuffles of the same span;
  - edits in matched nearby sequence with comparable GC content;
  - the unedited reference sequence.

The precise ALT strings and coordinates must come from a machine-readable
primary source; they must not be reconstructed from a figure by eye. The paper's
supplementary workbook publishes the sg1617 guide and processed-data sample
labels, but not the exact clone alleles. Consequently,
`experiments/interpretability/manifests/bcl11a_e58_sg1617_blocked_v1.json`
records this as a data-provenance blocker until an allele table or clone
sequences can be obtained.

### Outputs and contexts

Use K562 tracks because the local model metadata includes GATA1 ChIP-seq,
ATAC-seq, DNase-seq, H3K27ac/H3K4me1, PRO-cap, CAGE and RNA-seq. Report the
experimental-context mismatch explicitly: key editing experiments used HUDEP-2
or CD34-positive erythroid progenitors rather than K562.

Run 131,072-bp inputs first, then repeat every result that supports the final
claim with a 1,048,576-bp input. The shorter window barely contains the +58-kb
relationship and is a development aid, not a substitute for the full-context
result.

Expected output directions are reduced GATA1 binding, local PRO-cap/eRNA,
enhancer activity and BCL11A RNA. Accessibility and histone outputs are useful
corroboration but are not mandatory pass criteria because the experimental and
model cell contexts do not match exactly.

## Substantive evaluation 2: OpenSplice

OpenSplice measured more than 590,000 variants across 608 alternatively spliced
human exons, including exonic and proximal-intronic substitutions and 1-, 3-,
6- and 21-nt deletions. It was released in May 2026, after AlphaGenome's model
and original evaluation, making it the strongest available temporal holdout.

### Frozen sampling protocol

Before inspecting AlphaGenome internals:

1. Select five exons with reproducible wild-type PSI and complete genomic
   mappings. Prefer the OpenSplice curated examples and include at least one
   clinically familiar exon, but do not select exons based on AlphaGenome
   performance.
2. For each exon select ten high-confidence variants:
   - the three most negative measured delta-logit-PSI values;
   - the three most positive measured delta-logit-PSI values;
   - four variants with the smallest absolute delta-logit-PSI values, matched
     as closely as possible by position class and mutation type.
   Apply the authors' recommended barcode-count, replicate and error-model
   quality filters before ranking variants.
3. Freeze the resulting 50-variant manifest, including experimental PSI,
   uncertainty, genomic allele, exon boundaries and selection reason.
4. Run AlphaGenome in native-genome mode. Treat minigene-mode results as a
   sensitivity analysis, not a replacement for native genomic context.
5. Perform mechanistic analysis only on variants whose output direction
   AlphaGenome predicts correctly. Report the fraction rejected at this gate.

Start at AlphaGenome's smallest supported input for splice-site work and repeat
all final examples at 131,072 bp. The primary readouts are `splice_sites`,
`splice_site_usage` and the experimentally corresponding change in exon
inclusion. Junction and RNA outputs are secondary because a minigene PSI assay
does not reproduce a native tissue transcriptome.

OpenSplice already includes scripts that benchmark AlphaGenome's predictive
scores. The substantive contribution here is causal recovery of regulatory
features, not another variant-score benchmark.

## Frozen causal criteria

Freeze code, manifests, target outputs and these thresholds before evaluating
BCL11A or OpenSplice. Tooling controls may be used to debug code but not to tune
the substantive thresholds.

For a donor run whose internal state is patched into a recipient run, define
patch recovery as

```text
recovery = (patched_output - recipient_output)
           / (donor_output - recipient_output)
```

The donor and recipient are normally REF and ALT for loss-of-function edits,
and ALT and REF for gain-of-function edits. Thus positive recovery always means
movement toward the state supplying the patched activation. Do not calculate
this ratio for a target whose donor-versus-recipient effect is numerically
indistinguishable from zero.

### Required stage gates

1. **Output gate**
   - BCL11A: the primary edits must reduce predicted BCL11A RNA in K562.
   - OpenSplice: report signed prediction accuracy on all 30 high-effect
     variants; mechanistic recovery is evaluated only for correctly predicted
     variants.
2. **Localization gate**
   - BCL11A: the published GATA1-motif span must rank in the top 5% of matched
     local windows for input attribution or intervention effect.
   - OpenSplice: the recovered feature must overlap an experimentally mapped
     enhancer/silencer segment more often than position-matched controls.
3. **Sufficiency-like patch gate**
   - The predeclared candidate circuit must achieve median recovery of at least
     0.25 and exceed the 95th percentile of matched random interventions.
4. **Necessity-like ablation gate**
   - Ablating the same component in the donor/reference run must move the target
     output in the predicted direction and exceed the 95th percentile of the
     matched-null absolute effect.
5. **Specificity gate**
   - The intervention must affect the intended output more strongly than
     predeclared unrelated genes/tracks and must not simply suppress all model
     outputs.
6. **Replication gate**
   - A claimed mechanism must reproduce across at least two edits at BCL11A or
     at least three exons in OpenSplice. Single-locus anecdotes are labelled
     case studies, not general mechanisms.

### Failure definitions

The substantive hypothesis fails if any of the following occurs:

- AlphaGenome does not reproduce the expected output direction;
- proposed circuit effects do not beat matched random interventions;
- patching rescues the output but the converse ablation is ineffective;
- the apparent circuit changes unrelated outputs equally or more strongly;
- results depend on one context length, shift, orientation or checkpoint;
- only examples selected after viewing internal results support the claim.

A failure is reportable. It may mean AlphaGenome predicts through a shortcut,
uses a distributed mechanism not detected by the intervention, or does not
represent the experimental biology in the chosen cell context.

## Mandatory controls

### Sequence and biological controls

- sham and same-allele runs;
- length-, position-, GC- and mutation-type-matched edits;
- dinucleotide-preserving shuffles where a sequence ablation is required;
- known neutral OpenSplice variants;
- the biologically relevant track versus unrelated cell/tissue tracks;
- the intended target gene versus expressed non-target genes in the window.

### Internal-intervention controls

- random heads, layers and positions with the same intervention size;
- distance-matched pair edges;
- enhancer-to-non-target-promoter and control-region-to-target-promoter edges;
- patching REF into REF and ALT into ALT;
- reverse-complement and small input-shift consistency;
- all available fold checkpoints where practical.

Intervention indices must be runtime data rather than compilation constants
where possible, so null sweeps reuse one compiled executable.

## Leakage and interpretation limits

- AlphaGenome was trained on reference-genome assay tracks. Recovering a motif
  or assay feature at a familiar locus may reflect learned training data rather
  than biological discovery.
- TAL1 and COL6A2 were published AlphaGenome examples and are excluded from all
  substantive success counts.
- BCL11A predates AlphaGenome and its reference tracks may have contributed to
  training. Its perturbational readouts are valuable but not a clean temporal
  holdout. Use a checkpoint for which the locus is held out if the fold mapping
  permits, and report the mapping.
- OpenSplice is a temporal holdout, but its minigene measurements lack the full
  native cellular and chromatin context.
- Fulco/Gasperini and ENCODE-rE2G data have already been used to evaluate
  sequence-to-function models, including AlphaGenome. They test causal
  internals here, not predictive-score novelty.
- All selected examples, exclusions and failed predictions must be reported.
  Post-hoc examples must be clearly separated from the frozen evaluation.
- Attention magnitude is not accepted as a causal explanation without patching
  and converse ablation.
